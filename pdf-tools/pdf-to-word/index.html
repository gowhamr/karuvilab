How the PDF to Word Converter Works

Converting PDF files to Word format is a complex task because PDF is a fixed-layout format designed for printing, while Word is a flow-layout format designed for editing. KaruviLab's converter uses a secure, privacy-first approach by performing the conversion entirely within your browser.

The Extraction Process

Our tool utilizes PDF.js (the same engine that powers Firefox's PDF viewer) to parse the internal structure of your document. It systematically extracts every text element from each page, identifies line breaks, and attempts to reconstruct the paragraph structure. This raw text is then bundled into a modern .docx container.

Why Layout is Stripped

Professional server-side converters use expensive proprietary engines to replicate complex layouts. Because our tool runs locally on your device to ensure 100% privacy, it focuses on high-fidelity text extraction. This means while you get all the words from your PDF in an editable format, you will need to re-apply advanced formatting like columns, tables, and specific image placements.

Security and Data Sovereignty

Traditional "free" online converters often keep a copy of your uploaded documents for training their systems or for unknown purposes. With KaruviLab, your sensitive business reports, contracts, and personal documents never leave your RAM. Once you close the tab, all traces of the document are gone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can it convert scanned images to Word?

This version supports "native" PDFs (files created from Word, Google Docs, etc.). It does not currently include Optical Character Recognition (OCR). If your PDF is a set of photographs/scans of paper, this tool will not be able to extract the text.

Are there file size limits?

Since the processing happens in your browser's memory, we recommend files under 50MB for the best experience. Larger files may cause the browser tab to slow down or crash on mobile devices.

Will it handle encrypted PDFs?

If your PDF is password-protected, you will need to remove the protection first using our PDF Unlock tool before converting it to Word.

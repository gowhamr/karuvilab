About the Date Calculator

Find the number of days, weeks, months, or years between any two dates — or add/subtract a specific number of days from a start date.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can I calculate with this tool?

You can calculate the duration between two dates or add/subtract days from a specific date.

Does it include the end date?

You can usually choose whether to include the end date in the total duration calculation.

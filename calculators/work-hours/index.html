How the Work Hours Calculator Works

Keeping track of hours worked can be surprisingly complex, especially when accounting for variable start times, lunch breaks, and overtime. Our Work Hours Calculator simplifies this process by providing an easy-to-use interface for logging your daily activity and instantly seeing your totals in both standard and decimal formats.

Standard vs. Decimal Hours

Most employers and payroll software require time to be submitted in decimal format (e.g., 8.5 hours instead of 8 hours and 30 minutes). This tool automatically performs that conversion for you, ensuring that your timesheets are always accurate and ready for submission.

Break Deductions

Unpaid breaks are a common feature of the modern workday. By entering your break duration in minutes for each shift, the calculator automatically subtracts that time from your total duration. This prevents the common mistake of overestimating hours by forgetting to account for lunch or rest periods.

Income Estimation

If you work as a freelancer or contractor, you can enter your hourly rate to get an immediate estimate of your gross earnings for the period. This is helpful for budgeting and project management, giving you a real-time view of the value of your labor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate overnight shifts?

Yes. If your end time is earlier than your start time (e.g., 10:00 PM to 06:00 AM), the calculator automatically assumes the shift crossed into the next day and calculates the 8-hour duration correctly.

How do I clear the data?

Since the data is not stored, refreshing the page will reset all inputs to their defaults. We recommend copying your totals before closing the tab.

Is there a limit to the number of entries?

No. You can add as many rows as needed for a week, a month, or even a full project lifecycle. Performance is optimized for hundreds of entries.

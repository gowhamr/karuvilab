How KaruviLab Works

🔒 100% Client-Side Processing

KaruviLab is unique because it doesn't upload your files to any server. All processing—compressing, converting, and validating—happens directly in your browser's memory using modern technologies like WebAssembly and the Canvas API.

⚡ Instant Results

Since your files don't need to travel over the internet, there are no upload or download wait times. Processing is limited only by your device's speed.

🌍 Works Offline

Once the page is loaded, most tools work even without an internet connection. This makes it safe to use for sensitive documents like ID proofs and certificates.

🇮🇳 Built for Official Portals

We specifically design our tools to meet the strict requirements of government portals, online application systems, and official document submissions that have rigid size and format limits.

🤝 Open & Transparent

KaruviLab uses established open-source libraries to handle complex file formats. We don't track you, we don't show intrusive ads, and we don't store your data.

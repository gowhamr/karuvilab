How the Grammar & Spell Checker Works

Proofreading is an essential part of writing, but many online tools send your private drafts to distant servers for analysis. KaruviLab's Grammar & Spell Checker is different—it uses a library of common linguistic rules and dictionary patterns that run exclusively in your browser.

Rule-Based Detection

Our tool scans your text for common grammatical pitfalls, such as "it's" vs "its" confusion, duplicate words (e.g., "the the"), and frequently misspelled English words. By focusing on high-impact errors, it provides quick, reliable feedback without the overhead of heavy AI models.

Privacy-First Proofreading

Because the checker runs locally, you can use it for sensitive documents, private emails, and confidential reports without fear of your data being stored or used for training commercial AI. Your text exists only in your browser's temporary memory.

Instant Suggestions

When an error is detected, the tool provides a clear explanation and a one-click fix. This interactive approach helps you not only correct your text but also understand the underlying grammar rules to improve your writing over time.

Detected Categories

  • Spelling: Hundreds of commonly misspelled English words.
  • Punctuation: Missing spaces after commas or periods.
  • Grammar: Subject-verb agreement and common homophone errors.
  • Redundancy: Accidental word repetitions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this as powerful as Grammarly?

This tool is designed for speed and privacy rather than deep stylistic analysis. While it won't catch every subtle nuance like a multi-gigabyte AI model, it is perfect for catching common "fat-finger" typos and basic grammatical slips instantly.

Does it support languages other than English?

Currently, our ruleset is optimized for English (US/UK). Support for other languages may be added in future updates as we expand our local dictionaries.

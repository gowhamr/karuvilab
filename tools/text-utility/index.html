How the Text Utility Master Works

The Text Utility Master is designed to be your primary destination for quick text formatting and analysis. Whether you are a developer cleaning up code comments, a writer checking word counts, or a social media manager looking for eye-catching fonts, this tool handles it all locally in your browser.

1. Case Conversion

Convert your text into various formats instantly. UPPERCASE is useful for headings, lowercase for standardizing data, and Title Case for book or article titles. We also support programming conventions like camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case, making it a handy tool for naming variables or files.

2. Text Cleaning

Cleaning text manually is tedious. Our utility allows you to remove extra whitespace (double spaces, tabs), strip empty lines that often occur during copy-pasting, and remove specific character types like numbers or special symbols. The HTML stripper is particularly useful for extracting raw content from web snippets.

3. Advanced Word Counting

Beyond simple word counts, we provide character counts (with and without spaces), line counts, and an estimated reading time based on an average speed of 200 words per minute. This helps you gauge the length and readability of your content for different platforms.

4. Fancy Text Generation

Using Unicode character mapping, we transform your plain text into stylish variations. These are not real "fonts" but unique symbols that work across most social media platforms like Instagram, Twitter, and Facebook, allowing you to stand out without needing external CSS.

Example Usage

Input: " hello WORLD! 123 "

After 'Remove Extra Spaces': "hello WORLD! 123"

After 'Title Case': "Hello World! 123"

After 'Remove Numbers': "Hello World!"

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Like all KaruviLab tools, the Text Utility Master operates entirely in your browser. Your text never leaves your device, ensuring 100% privacy and security.

What characters does 'Remove Special Chars' target?

It removes non-alphanumeric characters, excluding basic spaces. This is ideal for cleaning up dirty data or preparing text for certain database imports.

Will the fancy text work on Instagram?

Yes, most of the styles generated use standard Unicode symbols that are widely supported by major social media platforms and messaging apps.

How the Task Reminder Works

In a world of complex project management software, sometimes you just need a simple place to jot down your immediate goals. KaruviLab's Task Reminder is designed for exactly that. It's a lightweight, "no-account" utility that helps you stay focused on your daily agenda without the clutter of cloud sync or subscriptions.

Persistent Browser Storage

This tool uses localStorage, a secure storage area provided by your web browser. This means your tasks are saved locally on your computer or phone. If you close the tab or restart your browser, your list will still be there when you return. However, because the data is local, it won't sync between different devices.

100% Privacy and Security

Your "To-Do" list often contains personal or sensitive information. Traditional apps store this on their servers. With our Task Reminder, your data never leaves your device. We have zero access to your tasks, ensuring your privacy is completely protected.

Simplicity by Design

We've stripped away the noise to focus on the essentials: adding tasks, marking them as complete, and removing them. The clean interface allows you to manage your day with minimal friction, making it an ideal companion for deep-work sessions where you want to minimize distractions.

Usage Best Practices

  • The Rule of Three: Try to limit your active list to the three most important tasks for the day to avoid feeling overwhelmed.
  • Be Specific: Instead of "Work on project," try "Draft first three slides of presentation."
  • Review & Clear: Use the "Clear Completed" button at the end of the day to celebrate your progress and start fresh tomorrow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I lose my tasks if I clear my browser cache?

Yes. Since the tasks are stored in localStorage, "Clearing Site Data" or "Clearing All History" in your browser settings will remove the saved tasks. We recommend using this for daily or short-term planning.

Can I set audio alerts?

This version is a visual task tracker. We are exploring browser notification support for future updates, which would allow for active alerts even if the tab is in the background.

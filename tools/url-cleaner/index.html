Why You Should Use a URL Cleaner

When you copy links from social media platforms, newsletters, or advertisements, they often come appended with "tracking parameters." These are strings of text after the ? in a URL that help marketers identify where you came from, what you clicked, and even your unique user ID.

Protect Your Privacy

Sharing links with tracking parameters like fbclid (Facebook Click ID) or gclid (Google Click ID) can sometimes leak information about your browsing habits to the recipient. A URL cleaner strips these identifiers, giving you a neutral link that preserves your privacy.

Cleaner, More Professional Links

Long, cluttered URLs look messy in documents, emails, and social media posts. By removing unnecessary bloat like utm_source, utm_medium, and utm_campaign, you make your links shorter and much more readable without using a third-party URL shortener that might expire or be blocked.

How It Works Locally

Our Link Cleaner works entirely in your browser. When you paste a URL, the tool uses the standard URL API to parse the string, identify known tracking parameters, and reconstruct a "clean" version of the address. No data is sent to KaruviLab servers, meaning your browsing history remains 100% private.

Example of Link Cleaning

Original: https://shop.com/item?id=99&utm_source=summer_sale&fbclid=xyz123

Cleaned: https://shop.com/item?id=99

Frequently Asked Questions

Will cleaning the link break it?

In 99% of cases, no. Tracking parameters are designed to be optional data for analytics. However, if a website incorrectly requires a specific parameter to function, the link might load differently. You can always uncheck "Strip All Query Params" if you suspect a specific ID is required.

What common parameters are removed?

By default, we target UTM params, FBCLID, GCLID, IID, MC_CID, RB_CLICKID, and several other common marketing tags used by major platforms.

Does this tool support affiliate links?

Some affiliate links use query parameters to track referrals. If you use this tool on an affiliate link, it may remove the referral ID, causing the affiliate not to get credited. Use with caution on such links.

Why Image SEO Matters?

Optimizing images is one of the most overlooked aspects of SEO. Properly named files and descriptive alt text help your content appear in Google Image Search results and improve overall page relevancy.

Optimization Tips

  • Be Specific: Use "vintage-brown-leather-satchel.jpg" instead of "bag.jpg".
  • No Stop Words: Avoid "a", "the", "and" in filenames.
  • Alt Text is Key: Describe the image as if you're explaining it to someone over the phone.

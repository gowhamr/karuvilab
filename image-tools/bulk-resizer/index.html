How the Bulk Image Resizer Works

The Bulk Image Resizer is a powerful client-side utility that leverages the modern Canvas API and File System access to process multiple images simultaneously. Instead of uploading your photos to a server, this tool uses your computer's own processing power to perform the transformations, keeping your data entirely private.

Fast Batch Processing

Whether you have 5 images or 50, our tool iterates through your selection, resizes them to your target width (maintaining original aspect ratio), and converts them to your chosen format. This is ideal for preparing large galleries for web use or optimizing assets for social media posts in one go.

Why Format Matters

Choosing the right format can significantly impact your file size. WebP is highly recommended for modern websites as it offers superior compression compared to JPEG. JPEG is best for compatibility, and PNG should be used when you need to preserve transparency (alpha channel).

Automatic ZIP Packaging

To make the workflow as efficient as possible, once all images are processed, the tool automatically packages them into a single .zip file. This prevents your browser from triggering dozens of individual download prompts and keeps your "Downloads" folder organized.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a limit to how many images I can resize?

The only limit is your device's memory. For most modern smartphones and computers, you can comfortably process up to 100 images at a time. For extremely large batches, we recommend processing in smaller groups.

Does it support HEIC images?

Yes, if your browser supports the HEIC format (common on Safari/Mac/iOS). For Chrome and Edge users, we recommend converting HEIC files to JPEG before using the bulk resizer, or using our specialized HEIC converter tool.

Will the quality be lower?

Resizing an image always involves some interpolation, but by setting the "Quality" slider to 80 or higher, the visual difference is usually imperceptible while the file size is drastically reduced.

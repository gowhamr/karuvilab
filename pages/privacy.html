Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

🔒

Privacy Promise: We believe your data is your own. KaruviLab is a "Serverless Utility" — every operation happens 100% on your device. We do not collect, store, or see your files.

1. 100% Local Processing

KaruviLab is built on the principle of client-side processing. Your files are never sent to a server. They stay in your device's memory until the process is complete.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Content Logging: We do not log the contents of your text, images, or documents.
  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
  • No Tracking: We do not use Google Analytics or tracking pixels.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

KaruviLab does not use cookies for tracking. We use the browser's localStorage only to remember your preferred theme (Light/Dark mode). This data never leaves your device.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our privacy-first approach, feel free to reach out to R Gowtham at wanderseven@proton.me.

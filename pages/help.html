Help & FAQ

How KaruviLab works, common questions, and quick troubleshooting.

How KaruviLab works

🔒 100% local processing

Every file you drop in is processed by your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

⚡ Instant results

Because there's no upload step, your output is ready as soon as your device finishes the calculation.

🌍 Works offline

After the page loads, most tools keep working without an internet connection — perfect for sensitive documents.

🇮🇳 Built for portals

Compressors and validators target the strict size, format, and resolution rules of government and exam portals.

Frequently asked questions

🔒 Is my data safe?

Yes, 100%. KaruviLab uses client-side JavaScript and WebAssembly to process files. Your documents never leave your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

🌍 Does it work without the internet?

Most tools work completely offline once the page has loaded. This is part of our commitment to privacy and performance. You'll need a connection only the first time you load a tool.

🇮🇳 Can I use this for government applications?

Yes. We specifically design our compressors and validators to meet the requirements of Indian government portals (SSO, Passport, Exam forms, etc.) and similar systems.

📂 What file types are supported?

Images: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, BMP, HEIC. Documents: PDF (compress, merge, split, convert). Code & text: JSON, CSV, Markdown, plain text. Specific tools list their supported formats on the upload zone.

📚 How do I save my output?

Each tool ends with a download button. Click it to save the result to your device — exactly like any normal download.

💾 Why does the app remember my recent tools?

The home page surfaces tools you've used recently for faster access. The data is stored only in your browser's localStorage and is never transmitted anywhere. You can clear it any time from ⚙️ Settings → Recent tools.

⚠️ A tool failed or behaved strangely. What now?

First, refresh the page — most issues clear up after a fresh load. If the problem persists, try the same tool with a smaller file, switch browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari), or send us a note via the contact page.

📱 Does it work on mobile?

Yes. KaruviLab is designed mobile-first. Some advanced PDF and image tools work best on devices with at least 2 GB of RAM, since processing happens in your browser.

Help & FAQ

How KaruviLab works, common questions, and troubleshooting.

How KaruviLab works

🔒 100% local processing

Every file you drop in is processed by your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

⚡ Instant results

Because there's no upload step, your output is ready as soon as your device finishes the calculation.

🌍 Works offline

After the page loads, most tools keep working without an internet connection — perfect for sensitive documents.

🇮🇳 Built for portals

Compressors and validators target the strict size, format, and resolution rules of government portals.

Frequently asked questions

🔒 Is my data safe?

Yes, 100%. KaruviLab uses client-side JavaScript to process files. Your documents never leave your device.

🌍 Does it work without the internet?

Most tools work completely offline once the page has loaded. You'll need a connection only the first time.

🇮🇳 Can I use this for government applications?

Yes. We specifically design our tools to meet the requirements of official portals and exam forms.

📂 What file types are supported?

Images (JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, etc.), PDFs, and various developer formats like JSON, CSV, and Markdown.

⚠️ A tool failed. What now?

First, refresh the page. If the problem persists, try a different browser or contact us via the contact page.
